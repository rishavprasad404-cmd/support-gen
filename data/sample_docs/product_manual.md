# ShopSphere Product Manual

## SphereBuds Pro (True Wireless Earbuds)
SphereBuds Pro (price: INR 7,999, available in Black and White) feature Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), transparency mode, and 11mm dynamic drivers. Battery life is up to 8 hours on a single charge and 32 hours total with the USB-C charging case. Rated IPX5 water-resistant against sweat and light rain.

## How to Pair and Reset SphereBuds Pro
1. Open the charging case lid with both earbuds inside; the LED will blink white to indicate pairing mode.
2. Select SphereBuds Pro in your phone's Bluetooth menu.
3. To factory reset SphereBuds Pro, place both earbuds in the open case and hold the case button for 10 seconds until the LED flashes red three times.

## SphereWatch Lite (Smartwatch)
SphereWatch Lite (price: INR 4,999) features a 1.78-inch AMOLED display, continuous heart-rate and SpO2 monitoring, sleep tracking, and 100+ sports modes. Battery life lasts up to 10 days on typical use and charges fully in 90 minutes via the magnetic charging cable. Rated IP68 dust and water-resistant up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes.
