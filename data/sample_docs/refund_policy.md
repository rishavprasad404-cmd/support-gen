# Return and Refund Policy

## 30-Day Return Window
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Items delivered more than 30 days ago are not eligible for return (though they may still be covered under the 1-year manufacturer warranty if defective).

## Return Eligibility Conditions
To qualify for a return within 30 days of delivery, the product must be in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals, charging cables, and tags intact. Used ear-tips on audio products are sanitized or replaced upon inspection.

## How to Initiate a Return
1. Provide your order ID (for example, SG1001) to customer support or open the My Orders page.
2. Select Return Item and choose the reason for return.
3. Our courier partner will schedule a free doorstep pickup within 48 hours.

## Refund Processing Time
Once the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes quality inspection, refunds are initiated within 2 business days. The amount is credited back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days (or via UPI/bank transfer for Cash on Delivery orders).
