# ShopSphere Frequently Asked Questions

## Payment Methods
ShopSphere accepts UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa, MasterCard, RuPay debit and credit cards, NetBanking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders under INR 10,000.

## Order Cancellation
You can cancel an order for a full refund any time before it is shipped (while the status is Processing). Once an order is In transit, it cannot be cancelled directly, but you may refuse delivery or initiate a return after delivery.

## Warranty Coverage
All ShopSphere electronic products, including SphereBuds Pro and SphereWatch Lite, come with a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship from the date of delivery. Physical damage, water abuse beyond the stated IP rating, and lost accessories are not covered under warranty.

## Customer Support Hours
Our human support team is available Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST. When our AI assistant escalates your query, a support ticket is created and a human representative replies via email within 24 business hours.
