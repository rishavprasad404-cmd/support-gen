# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Shipping Timelines and Charges
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days across India and is completely free on all orders above INR 999. For orders below INR 999, a flat shipping fee of INR 79 applies. Express shipping (1 to 2 business days) is available in metro cities for INR 149.

## Courier Partners and Tracking
ShopSphere ships orders through trusted national courier partners including BlueDart, Delhivery, and Ecom Express. Once your order leaves our warehouse, you receive a tracking number to check live shipment status.

## Delayed or Missing Packages
If your package status shows In transit past the expected delivery date, please allow up to 48 additional hours for courier network updates. If a package is marked Delivered but you have not received it, contact support within 72 hours so we can open a courier investigation.
